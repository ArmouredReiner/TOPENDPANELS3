What To Do After a Crash

We know how stressful it is to be in a car crash. But dont worry, if you dont know what to do or are uncertain of what steps you should take please contact Top End Panels and we will prepare everything for you.

When you’ve been involved in an accident, having the correct information ready helps speed up the repair and insurance process. But whether you have the information or not we will help you get back on the road

If possible you can bring the following:

  • Your driver’s licence
  • Your vehicle registration
  • The other party’s licence details
  • Their vehicle details (rego, make, model)
  • Any photos or videos of the crash scene and damages

The more information you provide, the faster we can assist in processing your claim.

Our Repair Process

Pulling & Structural Alignment

Once your vehicle arrives in our workshop, we follow a detailed and proven repair procedure to ensure your car is restored to factory condition.

  • Initial Assessment: We inspect and record all visible and structural damage.
  • Strip Down: Damaged panels and components are carefully removed.
  • Parts Replacement: New genuine parts are ordered and fitted.
  • Panel Work: Structural alignment and gap checks are performed to ensure precision.
  • Painting: Panels are prepped, masked, and painted in controlled conditions.
  • Reassembly & Finishing: Everything is refitted with final checks to guarantee a perfect finish.
